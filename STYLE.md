# Writing style guide

This is the editor's reference for matching Felix's voice. It's based on the posts written so far (`hello-world`, `bear-trap`) and should grow as he accepts or rejects edits.

## House rules (always apply)
- No Oxford (serial) commas: "products, data and golf", not "products, data, and golf".
- American spelling ("analyzing", "color").
- Write product and company names the official way: GitHub, BigQuery, OpenTofu.

## Voice
- First person and conversational, like explaining something to a curious friend.
- Honest about limits and struggles. He'll admit he had trouble with a config file or that something doesn't scale yet. Keep these moments; don't polish them into confidence.
- Dry, self-aware humor, often at the expense of a stereotype ("delusional wannabe pros", "podcast bros"). Don't add jokes, but don't cut his.
- Personal references are part of the voice: golf, Tiger Woods and quotes from people he admires.
- Occasional rhetorical questions ("What better way to answer that question than to collect and analyze the data yourself?").
- Some informal and British-leaning idioms ("not on the cards"). Leave them as they are.

## Sentences
- Mixes long, flowing sentences with short punchy ones. Don't break every long sentence up.
- Uses intensifiers and softeners like "really", "definitely" and "a little bit". Trim them only where they stack up or weaken a key point, not everywhere.
- Watch for semicolons used where a colon or dash belongs ("these learnings; a place to make..." should be a colon). Fix the punctuation but keep the sentence.

## Structure by series
**essay**: Opens with a concrete hook (a phrase, story or reference) and connects it to a personal point. Flowing paragraphs, no subheadings. Signs off with "Cheers,\nFelix".

**tech**: H2 sections, usually along the lines of *What it is*, *Why I built it*, *How it works*, *What I learned* and *What I want to learn next*. Explains the motivation before the technology. Ends by reflecting on lessons and next steps rather than with a summary.
